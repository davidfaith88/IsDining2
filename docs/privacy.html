IsDining Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Who we are

IsDining is a social dining app that helps people share meal plans with friends and lets restaurants send simple offers.

What information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Name you enter (or your Facebook name if you sign in with Facebook)
  • User ID created for your account on this device
  • Dining plans you create or join (restaurant name, time, optional note, who is going)
  • Groups you create
  • Restaurant owner details if you verify a business (restaurant name, optional email/phone)
  • Offers restaurants send and whether they were marked redeemed
  • Basic technical data needed to run the app (for example, through Firebase)

We do not sell your personal information.

How we use the information

We use this data to:

  • Show dining plans on the home feed
  • Let friends join plans (“I’m In”)
  • Let restaurant owners see relevant plans and send offers
  • Provide optional Premium owner insights (who joined, visit history, offer activity)
  • Operate, secure, and improve the app

Who can see your information

  • Other users may see plans you post publicly or share with groups/friends
  • Restaurant owners may see plans that match their restaurant, and Premium owners may see diner activity related to those plans
  • Service providers that power the app (such as Google Firebase) process data only to run the service
  • You can log out or reset local data on your device; you may also contact us to request deletion of account-related data we store

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

Facebook Login

If you choose Continue with Facebook, Facebook may share your public profile name and email with IsDining. We use that only to create or update your profile in the app. Facebook’s own policy also applies to data Facebook holds.

Data storage and security

Data is stored using cloud services (Firebase). We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is perfectly secure.

Children’s privacy

IsDining is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • Use name login instead of Facebook
  • Leave a plan (Undo I’m In)
  • Delete plans you created
  • Reset local data in Settings
  • Request access or deletion by contacting us

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
